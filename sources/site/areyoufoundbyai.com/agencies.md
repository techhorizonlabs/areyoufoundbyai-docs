## What you get

- **One console for the whole client list.** Worst performers first, so you open it to find problems rather than to browse.
- **Your brand on every report.** Your logo and name at the top; the measurement engine credited in small print.
- **A board pack per client, per month.** Six slides on where they stand, what moved and what is next, ready to print or send.
- **Scoped AI access per person.** Each staff member gets a key that reads only the clients they run, and you can revoke it in one click without touching anything else.
- **One MCP link for your team's AI.** Ask your own assistant which client moved this week and it answers from measured data, not memory.

## What it does not do

It is not keyword research and it is not a backlink index; keep the tool you already use for those. We measure what the answer engines say and hand your developers a specific task list. The method is open source at [github.com/techhorizonlabs/thl-open](https://github.com/techhorizonlabs/thl-open), so your team can check the scoring rather than take our word.

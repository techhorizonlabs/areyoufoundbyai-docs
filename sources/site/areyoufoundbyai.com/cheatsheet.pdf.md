Client emails & documents Everyday coding Research & analysis Website & marketing copy Opus 4.8 "the heavy lifter" SPEED ●●○○ (FAST MODE) · COST ●●●○ Deep work on big material. Reads entire codebases and contract stacks (1M-token context). Complex code & refactors Long documents, many files Proposals that must land Judgment calls with nuance Fable 5 "the frontier" SPEED ●●○○ · COST ●●●● The newest flagship family. Hardest reasoning, longest agent runs, best taste. Strategy & pricing decisions Agents that run for hours Gnarly multi-step problems "This really matters" work House rule: start on Sonnet. Drop to Haiku the moment the task feels mechanical. Escalate to Opus or Fable when being wrong is expensive — a lost client, a broken deploy, a mispriced deal. Low = instant answer. High = it works the problem first. This changes results more than most model swaps. E F F O R T W H E N F E E L S L I K E LOW Lookups, rewrites, formatting, anything you'll check yourself A fast colleague answering from memory MEDIUM / HIGH Debugging, planning, maths, anything multi-step Someone taking a walk to think it through first MAX The genuinely hard stuff — let it grind A specialist locking the door for an afternoon Cheat: a mid model on high effort often beats a big model on low effort for puzzles and debugging. But for taste — writing, strategy, judgment — the bigger model wins even on low. Puzzle → effort. Taste → model.

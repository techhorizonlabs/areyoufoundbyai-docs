Frequently asked questions

### What's the difference between AI Visibility and AI Readiness?

AI Visibility is whether AI names you in its answer right now. AI Readiness is how well your site is built for AI to find, trust and cite you. You can be ready but not yet visible, readiness is the lever that earns visibility.

### How is GEO different from SEO?

Traditional SEO targets blue links; GEO targets the AI's answer. The signals overlap, but AI weighs off-page entity, branded web mentions and third-party citations far more heavily than on-page keywords, that's what we measure.

### Which AI engines do you check?

ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI, queried directly, the way your customers ask them.

### Is the scan really free?

Yes, the scan is genuinely free. Your verdict, both scores and the receipt show instantly, with no signup and no email. If you want the results in your inbox, there's an optional "email me these results" link. Your full report, every answer word for word, across all seven engines, unlocks the moment your free trial starts, with nothing charged for 14 days.

### How long does the scan take?

About 60 seconds.

### Is there a guarantee on the paid report?

Yes. If the month-one human-reviewed report on Monitoring does not show you at least 5 specific things you did not already know, reply to the delivery email within 7 days for a full refund. The full report, included in month one of Monitoring, carries the same promise at 5.

### Does it work outside Australia?

Yes, the scan localises to your market automatically: it reads your site, works out where you operate, and asks the questions your local buyers ask (a Texas plumber gets "plumbing repair Bastrop TX" and Texan rivals).

## AI visibility in Brisbane, Melbourne, Sydney, Adelaide, and every Australian city

City questions are where AI recommendations bite hardest. When a buyer asks ChatGPT or Google's AI for "the best physio in Brisbane" or "a conveyancer in Adelaide", the engine assembles a short, city-specific list from local sources: Google Business profiles, local directories, review counts, and whoever gets talked about in that city. National SEO does not put you on that list.

The measurement works the way those buyers do. The free scan reads your locality straight from your own site and asks the engines the questions your city's buyers actually ask, so a Brisbane business is measured against Brisbane answers, a Perth business against Perth answers. The leaderboards above are Australia-wide; your own scan is local to you.

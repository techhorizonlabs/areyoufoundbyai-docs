# About Are you found by AI?

The AI-visibility scanner built by [Tech Horizon Labs](https://techhorizonlabs.com) in Noosa, Australia. We also build [PaddockMap](https://paddockmap.com.au), a local-food directory mapping Australian farm gates and markets, and the [THL Academy](https://academy.techhorizonlabs.com) — we practise the visibility work we measure.

Found by AI answers one question for a business: **when your customers ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Google's AI who to trust, are you in the answer?** The scanner queries the AI engines live with the questions real buyers ask, two engines on the free scan (ChatGPT and Gemini, twice each), seven on the full report, measures whether you're named, and shows who's named instead. Nothing is estimated: every number in every report comes from a live engine answer, a public registry, or a named data source.

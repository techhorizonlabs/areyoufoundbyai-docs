Get onto the platforms already feeding your category's AI answers: Reddit, cited 46× in the answers we measured, real answers in your category's subreddits, from a real profile; YouTube, cited 85× in the answers we measured, a channel with a few plain answer videos and community posts. Each of these is a source the engines draw on in your market right now, and you have no verified presence there yet.
3. Add direct quotations, a line from a named expert, founder or customer. Pages with quotations are cited markedly more often by AI assistants (~+40% visibility in the GEO study, arXiv:2311.09735).

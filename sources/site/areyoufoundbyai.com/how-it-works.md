**CH 05**The weekly diff

## Then we measure the change, not the vibe.

Monitoring re-asks **the same pinned questions every week**, so your trend line is real movement rather than freshly re-generated questions pretending to be a trend. When you change your site and a question flips, the weekly email says so: the change you shipped, the question that moved, the engines that now name you.

- **SOUNDER asks every question more than once.** Twice per engine on the free scan; up to three times with a majority vote on the full measure, up to 228 live answers.
- **BOSUN halts degraded scans instead of scoring them.** If we couldn't read your site or reach an engine, the report says so instead of printing a confident wrong number.
- **LOG freezes reports as measured, they never silently re-score.** Methodology upgrades are labelled, so a scoring change never masquerades as your movement.

## Common questions

### How do I check if AI recommends my business?

Paste your website into the free scanner. It asks the engines the questions your customers ask — ChatGPT and Google's Gemini on the free scan, all seven on a deep measure — and tells you whether you're named, or which competitor is named instead.

### How does the AI visibility scan work?

It reads your website, works out who your customers are, then asks the engines your buyers' questions, each sampled more than once. It scores you across 30+ signals into two numbers: AI Visibility (are you named now) and AI Readiness (is your site built to be found).

### Which AI engines does it check?

The free scan sounds ChatGPT and Google's Gemini. Deep measures cover seven: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek — and Google's AI Overviews gets its own column.

### Why do AI visibility scores change between scans?

AI engines are probabilistic — the same question can get different answers on different runs. That's why we sample every question multiple times (twice per engine on the free scan, three times with a majority vote in the full report) and why monitoring re-measures the same pinned questions each week. Genuine movement shows as a trend; single-run wobble doesn't.

### Does it work outside Australia?

Yes — the scan localises to your market automatically. It reads your site, works out where you operate, and asks the questions your local buyers ask: a Texas plumber gets Texas questions and Texan rivals, a Cardiff dog-walker gets Cardiff's. The public benchmark index is Australian because that's where we started measuring; more countries are being measured now.

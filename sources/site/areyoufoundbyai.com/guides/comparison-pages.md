# Win your own comparison queries

When a buyer searches "_you_ vs _rival_" or "_you_ alternatives", they are minutes from a decision, and [our analysis of 1,001 high-intent SERPs](https://areyoufoundbyai.com/research/high-intent-serps) found that **67% of "vs" searches and 84% of "alternatives" searches are won by a page the brand didn't write**. A rival's spin, an affiliate's listicle, an aggregator. The same pages feed AI assistants when someone asks "should I use _X_ or _Y_?". This playbook is drawn from the 274 "vs" winners we measured.

## 1\. Get your page list from reality

Don't guess which comparisons matter, use the rivals AI and search actually put next to you. Your [free scan](https://areyoufoundbyai.com/?src=guide-compare) names the competitors AI recommends instead of you; each one is a "_you_ vs _them_" page. Add your own "alternatives to _you_" page, the intent you're least likely to own today (16% brand-owned).

## 2\. Build each page like the winners build them

- **Title carries the query**: "_You_ vs _Rival_: \[honest frame\]". 79% of winners have the intent token in the title; 74% use a superlative ("best for…"), earn it, don't just claim it.
- **H1 close to the query**, not stuffed, winners average a near-match, only 23% exact-match.
- **An honest both-ways table early**: features, pricing, support, who each product is genuinely better for. The page that reads fair is the page that gets cited.
- **Say who should pick THEM.** One section, real reasons. It's what separates a comparison from an ad, and it's the part AI quotes.
- **CTA above the fold** (76% of winners), a trial or demo link, not a popup.
- **Visible "last updated" date** you actually honour (33% of winners show one); a table of contents once it's long (19%).
- **Schema: Article or WebPage is what winners use.** FAQPage appeared on just 7%, add a short real FAQ block if useful (10% do), skip it otherwise. Never fake review stars on your own comparison.

## 3\. The "alternatives" listicle is its own shape

For "alternatives to _you_", winners are numbered, dated roundups: **79% have a number in the title, 50% a year, 47% a visible update date**. Write "7 _YourBrand_ alternatives (and who they're right for)" yourself, name real competitors, be generous, state plainly where you're not the fit. Counter-intuitive and it works: you control the frame, you earn the citation, and AI learns the comparison from a page that includes you fairly.

## 4\. Ship it, then make it findable

Link the pages from your footer or a /compare hub, submit them with [IndexNow](https://areyoufoundbyai.com/tools/indexnow) the day they go live (Bing's index is what ChatGPT search reads), and keep names consistent with the rest of your entity signals.

## 5\. Measure whether it moved

Comparison visibility is measurable: re-scan after the pages land, and if you're monitoring, watch the "vs" questions in your weekly measure. Growth's rival share-of-voice shows the head-to-head trend directly.

**The honesty rules.** No fabricated ratings or review schema on pages you wrote about yourself. No copied "vs template" farms, engines have seen ten thousand of them. No trashing the rival: the measured winners are comparison pages, not hit pieces, and the fair page is the durable one. If the honest answer is "they're better for most people", win a different comparison.

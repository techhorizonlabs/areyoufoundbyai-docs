# Scoped access keys: your team's AI, per client

Your network page is a live MCP server. Add its URL to Claude, Cursor or any MCP client and your AI reads every client's scores, movement, mentions and fix plans mid-conversation. Scoped keys extend that to your team without handing everyone everything.

## What a key is

A key is a URL. Whoever holds it can connect their AI to the sites that key covers, and nothing else. There is no login and no password to manage; the URL is the credential, so it is shared like one.

## What a key sees

- **Only the sites you tick** when you create it, or the whole network if you choose that, which then follows new sites automatically.
- Scope is checked against live membership on every call: remove a site from your network and every key loses it at the same moment.
- Keys are read-only. They cannot change settings, add or remove sites, touch billing, or see your fleet page.

## Minting and handing over

On your network page, open **AI access**. Name the key after the person, tick their clients, create. Then press **Copy setup** next to the key: it copies a complete handoff message, with the URL and the exact steps for the Claude app, Claude Code and Cursor, ready to paste into an email or chat.

## Revoking and auditing

Each key shows how many times it has been used and when it was last used, so a key that should be active and is not tells you something too. Revoke is one click and takes effect immediately; nothing else changes.

## What your team member asks their AI

Once connected, the useful questions are plain ones: which of my clients moved this week, what should we do for this client this month, draft a post brief for that client from the measured data. The AI answers from live measurements, not memory.

The model in one line: the network link is yours, scoped keys are theirs, and every key can be watched and killed without touching anything else.

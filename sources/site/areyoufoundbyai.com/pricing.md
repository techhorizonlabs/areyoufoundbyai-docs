## Pricing — common questions

### Is the scan really free?

Yes — no card, no signup, and your verdict with both scores shows in about 60 seconds. Fair use applies: up to **15 free scans per browser per day**, and on very busy days a server-wide cap can queue new scans — leave your email and your result arrives as soon as it clears. The paid tiers are never affected by either cap.

### Is GST included?

Prices are in US dollars and exclude GST. Australian customers are charged 10% GST at checkout — so US$79/mo appears as US$86.90. Customers outside Australia pay the listed price with no tax added. Your tax invoice is issued automatically.

### Is there a guarantee?

Yes. The month-one human-reviewed report on Monitoring: at least 5 specific things you did not already know, or reply within 7 days for a full refund. No forms, no hoops.

### What's the AI-traffic beacon?

A one-line snippet you paste on your site that counts **real visitors arriving from AI** — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot and Gemini — with a live dashboard. No cookies, no personal data. Included with Monitoring; it's how you see the fixes pay off.

### Do you offer implementation?

Yes — done-for-you, custom-quoted on your needs: we implement your exact fix plan, or build white-label and multi-location setups for agencies. Use the form above; we reply within one business day and book a 15-minute call once it's a fit.

### Can I cancel Monitoring?

One click, from your console or any email, any time. No contracts, no exit forms. Your locked rate holds for as long as you're subscribed.

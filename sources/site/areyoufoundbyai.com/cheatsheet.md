# Which Claude do I use? 
And how do I make it know my business?

The one-pager we give every client. Four models, one effort dial, three ways to load your business in. Save it, share it, argue with it. Updated 22 Jul 2026.

01 · The four models

Haiku 4.5"the sprinter"SPEED ●●●● · COST ●○○○

Near-instant and the cheapest. Use it when the task is mechanical and volume is high.

- Sorting & tagging anything
- Pulling data out of emails/PDFs
- Meeting notes → summary
- Quick lookups & drafts

Sonnet 5"the workhorse"SPEED ●●●○ · COST ●●○○

The everyday default. Best smarts-per-dollar for 80% of real work.

- Client emails & documents
- Everyday coding
- Research & analysis
- Website & marketing copy

Opus 4.8"the heavy lifter"SPEED ●●○○ (FAST MODE) · COST ●●●○

Deep work on big material. Reads entire codebases and contract stacks (1M-token context).

- Complex code & refactors
- Long documents, many files
- Proposals that must land
- Judgment calls with nuance

Fable 5"the frontier"SPEED ●●○○ · COST ●●●●

The newest flagship family. Hardest reasoning, longest agent runs, best taste.

- Strategy & pricing decisions
- Agents that run for hours
- Gnarly multi-step problems
- "This really matters" work

**House rule:** start on **Sonnet**. Drop to **Haiku** the moment the task feels mechanical. Escalate to **Opus** or **Fable** when being wrong is expensive, a lost client, a broken deploy, a mispriced deal.

02 · The effort dial (most people never touch it)

Every model also has a **thinking / effort setting**: how long it reasons before answering. Low = instant answer. High = it works the problem first. This changes results more than most model swaps.

| Effort | When | Feels like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LOW | Lookups, rewrites, formatting, anything you'll check yourself | A fast colleague answering from memory |
| MEDIUM / HIGH | Debugging, planning, maths, anything multi-step | Someone taking a walk to think it through first |
| MAX | The genuinely hard stuff, let it grind | A specialist locking the door for an afternoon |

**Cheat:** a mid model on **high effort** often beats a big model on low effort for puzzles and debugging. But for taste, writing, strategy, judgment, the bigger model wins even on low. **Puzzle → effort. Taste → model.**

03 · Teach it your business (this is the unlock)

STEP 1**Make a Project, load your gold**In claude.ai: sidebar → Projects → new. Upload your website copy, your 2–3 best proposals, 5 real customer emails. Then set instructions: "Write like these examples. This is our tone, our offer, our prices." Every chat in the project now knows your business.

📁 Projects\+ Add to project knowledge⚙ Set custom instructions

STEP 2**Connect your real tools**Settings → Connectors. Plug in Gmail, Drive, Calendar (and any MCP tool your systems expose). Now it answers from your actual emails and files: "Summarise every open quote from this month's email."

🔌 ConnectorsConnected: Gmail🔍 Search & tools

STEP 3**Show, don't describe**The 3-example rule: paste two or three gold-standard outputs before you ask for a new one. Models copy patterns far better than they follow adjectives. "Professional but friendly" loses to three real examples, every time.

04 · When to use what, pin this bit

| The task | Model | Effort | Plus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Label / sort / extract from 1,000 things | Haiku 4.5 | LOW | Clear format example |
| Reply to a customer email | Sonnet 5 | LOW | 3 past replies as examples |
| Write a proposal or quote | Opus 4.8 | MEDIUM | Project with your winners loaded |
| Debug code / fix a spreadsheet formula | Sonnet 5 → Opus 4.8 | HIGH | Paste the error, not a description |
| Read a 100-page contract / whole codebase | Opus 4.8 | MEDIUM | 1M context, give it everything |
| Pricing, strategy, "what should we do?" | Fable 5 | MAX | Real numbers in, always |
| An agent that works for hours unattended | Fable 5 / Opus 4.8 | HIGH | Connectors + tight instructions |
| Meeting → summary → action list | Haiku 4.5 | LOW | Your action-list template |

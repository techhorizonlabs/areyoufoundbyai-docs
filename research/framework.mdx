When someone asks ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity for "the best \[category\] in \[your city\]," a handful of businesses get named and the rest are invisible. That outcome isn't random, it's driven by six measurable signals. The THL AI-Visibility Framework defines those signals, weights them by their proven impact on real AI citations, and gives you a reproducible score so you can track improvement over time. The methodology is open-source at [github.com/techhorizonlabs/thl-open](https://github.com/techhorizonlabs/thl-open).

## The Six Dimensions

Every AI Readiness Score is a weighted composite of six dimensions. Here is what each one measures, why it matters, and how to earn it.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="AI Citability: 25% (highest weight)">
    **What it measures:** How quotable and self-contained your content is, how easily an AI can lift a clear, factual passage straight into its answer.

    **Why it matters:** AI answers are assembled from quotable passages. Thin, vague, or purely promotional copy gives the model nothing to use, so it reaches for a competitor who wrote a clear answer instead.

    **How to earn it:** Answer real buyer questions directly, lead with the conclusion, use headings, lists, statistics, and specifics. Write the paragraph you'd want the AI to quote verbatim.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Brand Authority: 20%">
    **What it measures:** Whether AI recognises you as a real, distinct entity, and whether trusted third parties (Wikipedia, Wikidata, Reddit, YouTube, review platforms, industry directories) reference you.

    **Why it matters:** AI recommends what others vouch for. A business with no third-party footprint reads as unknown and risky to name, however polished its own site. Brand Authority is, by a wide margin, the strongest predictor of AI visibility in our own data.

    **How to earn it:** Earn citations off your own domain: authoritative directory listings, industry-association profiles, genuine reviews and press mentions, a consistent entity (same name, address, links) everywhere, and (where realistic for your size) a Wikipedia or Wikidata entry.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Content E-E-A-T: 20%">
    **What it measures:** Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trust. Author credentials, a real About page, visible contact details, content provenance, and a privacy policy.

    **Why it matters:** Recommending a business is a trust decision. AI is built to avoid endorsing the untrustworthy, so it favours sites that prove who's behind them.

    **How to earn it:** Name your people and their credentials, show how you know what you claim, and make it obvious you're a real, contactable operation with a physical address or a verifiable business identity.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Technical GEO: 15%">
    **What it measures:** Whether AI crawlers can cleanly access and read your site, HTTPS, sitemaps, canonical URLs, mobile-friendliness, security headers, server-side rendering, and Core Web Vitals.

    **Why it matters:** If your content only appears after heavy JavaScript executes, or your crawler is blocked or timing out, none of the other signals matter because the AI never sees them.

    **How to earn it:** Serve content in the initial HTML response, keep pages fast and crawlable, and fix the plumbing. Your platform choice determines how easily you can do this.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Schema & Structured Data: 10%">
    **What it measures:** Structured data (JSON-LD) (Organization, LocalBusiness, Product, FAQ) that states what you are, where you operate, and what you offer in machine-readable form.

    **Why it matters:** Schema removes guesswork. It hands AI a clean, unambiguous fact sheet about your entity instead of making it infer everything from prose. On its own it is necessary, not sufficient. Our data shows why below.

    **How to earn it:** Add valid Organization and LocalBusiness JSON-LD with `sameAs` links to your verified profiles. Deliver it server-side or via edge injection, client-side JavaScript injection is invisible to AI crawlers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Platform Optimization: 10%">
    **What it measures:** Readiness for each AI surface, crawler access (robots.txt permissions for GPTBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot), a published `llms.txt`, and answer-shaped content tuned to how each engine retrieves.

    **Why it matters:** Each assistant sources answers differently. Quietly blocking AI crawlers, or never telling them what you do, guarantees you're absent from that surface entirely.

    **How to earn it:** Open the right crawlers, publish an `llms.txt`, and structure pages as direct answers to the questions customers actually ask AI.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The GEO Score Formula

Your **AI Readiness Score** (0–100) is a weighted composite of the six dimensions above:

```
AI Readiness Score =
  (AI Citability  × 0.25) +
  (Brand Authority × 0.20) +
  (Content E-E-A-T × 0.20) +
  (Technical GEO   × 0.15) +
  (Schema & Data   × 0.10) +
  (Platform Optim. × 0.10)
```

Your **AI Visibility Score** (0–100) is measured separately and independently. It reflects how often AI engines actually name your business when real buyer questions are asked. Readiness predicts potential; Visibility measures outcome.

## AI Visibility vs AI Readiness

These are two different questions with two different scores. Most "SEO audits" conflate them, which is why they fail to predict whether AI actually recommends you.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI Visibility Score" icon="eye">
    **Are you in the answer right now?**

    Whether AI assistants actually name or cite you when a real customer asks. Measured live across up to seven engines, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, and Google AI Overviews. This is the outcome that wins customers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Readiness Score" icon="gauge">
    **Is your site built to earn AI recommendations?**

    How well your site and off-site presence support being recommended, five site signals plus your external entity footprint, each scored 0–100. You can be highly ready yet still invisible: a well-built site that AI doesn't yet know or trust.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The gap between the two scores is the work. Readiness is what you control; Visibility is what it earns you. Our corpus of 2,354+ businesses shows an average Readiness of 67/100 alongside an average Visibility of 40.8/100, a 26-point gap that represents real citation opportunity being left on the table.

The most common way this gap manifests: a technically excellent site with no third-party footprint. The AI can read you perfectly; it just has no reason to trust or name you. Off-page brand recognition closes that gap. On-page improvements alone cannot.

## Key Research Findings from 2,354 Businesses

Found by AI has measured over 3,268 scored scans across distinct businesses. These are live measurements (not a survey, not a sample) from every business that has run a scan. The findings below recompute continuously from the live index.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="11× Visibility Gap" icon="chart-bar">
    Businesses scoring ≥70 on Brand Authority average **59.5** AI Visibility. Businesses scoring below 40 on Brand average just **5.3**, an eleven-times gap driven entirely by off-page recognition.
  </Card>
  <Card title="24% Score Near Zero" icon="eye-slash">
    **24% of measured businesses** score effectively invisible to AI (≤10/100) when a customer asks who to trust in their category. Most owners have never checked even once.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2-Point Technical Gap" icon="wrench">
    The Technical GEO gap between AI-named and AI-ignored businesses is just **2 points** (80 vs 78). Invisible businesses are as technically sound as named ones.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Brand Authority buckets, the real distribution:**

| Brand Score | Average AI Visibility |
|---|---|
| Brand ≥70 (strong third-party recognition) | **59.5 / 100** |
| Brand 40–69 (moderate recognition) | **33.0 / 100** |
| Brand &lt;40 (limited recognition) | **5.3 / 100** |

**The 41-point brand recognition gap:** Among businesses AI names consistently versus businesses it ignores, the Brand Authority gap is 41 points (79 vs 38). The E-E-A-T gap is a real but smaller 16 points (75 vs 59). Technical SEO and Schema are essentially flat.

**Schema alone shows near-flat results:** Across every schema-score bucket in our corpus, AI Visibility ranges from 42 to 47.5, nearly flat. Schema helps AI read you correctly; it does not make AI recommend you. This is why we score it as a 10% dimension and are transparent about it: schema is table stakes, not a lever. Google's own May 2026 guidance reached the same conclusion independently.

## The SEO Tier List Reality Check

A widely-shared 2026 SEO tier list ranks tactics from S to F. We checked every tier against our live corpus of 3,283 businesses. Here is what the data actually shows:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="What moves AI naming">
    **Off-page brand recognition (S-tier, confirmed):** The largest gap in our data. Businesses AI names average 79/100 on off-page brand signals versus 38/100 for invisible businesses, a 41-point gap. Being talked about elsewhere is the strongest measured predictor of being named.

    **Entity anchoring:** Consistent name, address, and entity signals across every platform help AI recognise you as a real, distinct entity rather than a generic description. Generic-word brand names create entity collisions our scanner flags.

    **Buyer-question-shaped pages (programmatic SEO, A-tier, confirmed):** Engines retrieve pages shaped like the questions buyers type. Use-case and comparison pages at scale match that shape.

    **JS-free HTML for crawlers (Technical, table stakes):** GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot fetch raw HTML. Content that only appears after JavaScript executes is invisible to the major AI crawler families.

    **Multi-sample measurement:** AI answers vary ask-to-ask. Schulte et al. (2026) confirm that AI-search visibility must be measured as a distribution across repeated queries, a single snapshot proves nothing. Found by AI multi-samples and majority-votes every question for this reason.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="What does NOT move AI naming">
    **Keyword stuffing (confirmed harmful):** Ranked at the bottom of every tier list, and our data confirms it costs more than rankings. One measured business's keyword-stuffed homepage title corrupted how AI reads its brand name, making every mention-counting system struggle to attribute it. Keyword stuffing now actively damages your entity.

    **Schema markup alone (C-tier, confirmed):** The schema gap between named and invisible businesses is just 10 points (76 vs 66). Schema helps engines read you correctly and does very little to make them recommend you.

    **Exact-match domains (S-tier claimed, unmeasured):** No one sharing the tier list has published a measurement of EMD lift for AI visibility. What we measure cuts the other way: generic-word names create entity collisions that hurt attribution.

    **Technical SEO beyond baseline (diminishing returns):** The technical gap between AI-named and AI-ignored businesses is 2 points. Fast, crawlable sites are table stakes, but beyond that, no amount of technical polish substitutes for off-page authority.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Are you found by AI? is built and operated by Tech Horizon Labs at [areyoufoundbyai.com](https://areyoufoundbyai.com). It asks the questions your buyers ask AI engines, saves the answers, and shows whether the engines name your business and who they name instead. The free scan asks ChatGPT and Gemini. The full measurement covers seven engines: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek and Google AI Overviews. Paste in your website, wait about a minute, and read the answers behind the result.

## What you get

| Deliverable | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Named share** | The headline measure: countable saved answers that name your business, divided by all countable saved answers. Named in 7 of 28 countable answers is 25%. |
| **Saved answers** | The answers each engine returned, question by question, with the sources returned with them. |
| **Who AI names instead** | The businesses the engines named instead of you, and how often. |
| **Site checks** | AI Readiness (out of 100) scores how well your website itself is set up to be understood by AI. Footprint (out of 100) scores your off-site presence separately. |
| **Fix plan** | A prioritised plan that turns the measurement into specific work, with generated schema and llms.txt you can use where they fit your site. |

Read the named share with the questions, engines, date and answer count beside it. A short free scan is a smaller sample than a full check.

<Note>
  Reports and the API also carry an **AI Visibility score** out of 100. It is an older weighted diagnostic score. It is not a percentage of AI recommendations, and it cannot be converted into a named share. See [AI Visibility vs AI Readiness](/concepts/visibility-vs-readiness).
</Note>

### AI Readiness signals

AI Readiness is measured from your site alone and is composed of five site signals: **Citability** (quotable content), **E-E-A-T** (trust and credibility), **Technical** (site health), **Schema** (machine-readable business facts) and **Platform** (AI-ready setup). A signal that could not be verified is left out rather than scored zero, and each report states the share each signal carries. Your off-site Footprint is scored beside Readiness, not inside it. See [AI Readiness and Footprint](/concepts/geo-score).

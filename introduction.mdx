Found by AI is an AI-visibility platform built by Tech Horizon Labs that tells you whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, and Google AI Overviews recommend your business when buyers ask questions in your category. It does not estimate or model anything: every number comes from a live engine answer, a public registry, or a named data source. Paste in your domain, wait about sixty seconds, and you'll see who the engines name — and who they name instead of you.

## What you get

Every scan produces two scores and a set of deliverables that work together:

| Deliverable | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **AI Visibility Score** (0–100) | Are you being named by AI engines right now? |
| **AI Readiness Score** (0–100) | Is your site built to earn AI recommendations? Composite of 6 dimensions. |
| **Verbatim receipts** | The exact words each engine said, question by question — not a chart, the actual answers. |
| **Competitor share-of-voice** | Which rivals the engines name instead of you, and how often. |
| **Fix plan** | Prioritised actions ranked by impact, with copy-paste schema and llms.txt generated for your entity. |

### AI Readiness Score dimensions

The Readiness Score is a composite of six weighted dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---|
| AI Citability | 25% |
| Brand Authority | 20% |
| Content E-E-A-T | 20% |
| Technical GEO | 15% |
| Schema & Structured Data | 10% |
| Platform Optimization | 10% |

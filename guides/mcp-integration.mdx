The Found by AI MCP integration turns your AI assistant into a live window onto your visibility measurements. Instead of copying scores out of your dashboard and pasting them into a chat, your assistant queries the data directly — mid-conversation, whenever the question comes up, always returning this week's numbers rather than a snapshot from whenever you last checked. This guide walks you through getting your endpoint and connecting it to every major AI tool.

## Get your MCP endpoint

Your MCP endpoint is available as soon as you have a monitoring subscription. To find your token URL:

1. Log in to your Found by AI console.
2. Go to **Settings** in the sidebar.
3. Copy your personal MCP endpoint URL — it looks like this:

```
https://areyoufoundbyai.com/mcp/YOUR-TOKEN
```

The token in the URL is your credential. Treat it like a password — share it with your own AI tools and trusted teammates, but do not post it in public repositories or shared documents.

<Note>
  **No subscription yet?** Test the MCP integration right now using our demo endpoint, which is connected to our own live monitoring of areyoufoundbyai.com: `https://areyoufoundbyai.com/mcp/demo`. It has 13 read-only tools available — the `request_rescan` action is disabled on the demo endpoint.
</Note>

## Connect to Claude (Desktop)

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http found-by-ai https://areyoufoundbyai.com/mcp/YOUR-TOKEN
```

Replace `YOUR-TOKEN` with the token from your Settings page. After running this command, any Claude Code session inside your repository can query your measurements directly.

## Connect to Claude (Projects)

Claude Projects support persistent instructions that apply to every new conversation you start inside the project. This means your assistant starts each chat already knowing your current scores — without you having to paste anything.

Create or open a Project in Claude, then find its **Custom instructions** or **Project knowledge** field (the exact label changes as Claude updates). Paste the following standing instruction, replacing the token with your own:

```
At the start of every new conversation in this project, fetch https://areyoufoundbyai.com/monitor/YOUR-TOKEN/context.md and use it as my current Found by AI data — my AI-visibility score, tracked buyer questions, priority fixes, and citation targets — before answering anything about my AI visibility, competitors, or what to do next. Re-fetch it fresh every new chat; do not rely on a cached copy from an earlier conversation.
```

Alternatively, connect your MCP endpoint via **Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector** in the Claude app and paste your MCP URL. With a live MCP connection, your assistant can query measurements mid-conversation, not just at the start.

## Connect to ChatGPT Projects

ChatGPT Projects support custom instructions that apply to every new conversation within the project. Open a Project in ChatGPT and paste the same standing instruction into the project's instructions field, using your monitor URL (`https://areyoufoundbyai.com/monitor/YOUR-TOKEN.json` for the JSON format or `/context.md` for plain text).

<Note>
  If you want Claude to query your data on demand mid-conversation (rather than only at the start), that requires an Action wired to the JSON endpoint, which is additional setup. Reach out at hello@techhorizonlabs.com if you want help configuring this.
</Note>

## Connect to Perplexity Spaces

Perplexity Spaces support custom instructions the same way Projects do. Create a Space dedicated to your business, open its settings, and paste the standing instruction into the custom instructions field using your monitor context URL. Every new conversation in that Space starts with your latest measurements fetched automatically.

## Connect to Gemini Gems

Gemini Gems support a persistent instructions field. Create a new Gem for your business monitoring workflow and paste the standing instruction into the instructions field. Look for **Instructions**, **Custom instructions**, or **Project knowledge** depending on the current Gemini interface.

## Connect to Cursor

In Cursor, add a custom MCP server with HTTP transport. No API key field is needed — the token embedded in the URL is the entire credential. Use:

```
https://areyoufoundbyai.com/mcp/YOUR-TOKEN
```

Or use the one-click **Add to Cursor** button in your console's **Connect your AI** section, which pre-fills the configuration for you.

## What you can ask

Once your assistant is connected, you can ask it anything you would ask a colleague who had your console open. The live connection means these questions work mid-conversation, weeks after initial setup, and always answer from current data:

<Accordion title="Score and trend questions">
- "What is my current AI visibility score for example.com?"
- "What moved in my AI visibility this week?"
- "Show me my readiness subscores and compare them to last week."
- "What is my off-site Footprint score and how does it compare to my overall readiness?"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Competitor and market questions">
- "Which competitors are being named instead of me?"
- "What is my share of voice against my top rivals?"
- "Which buyer questions am I invisible on, and who is AI naming instead of me on those?"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Fix plan and action questions">
- "What should I fix first?"
- "Read my fix plan and classify which items I can apply in my repository and which ones I need to handle manually."
- "What are the sources the engines actually cite in my category, and which ones do I appear on?"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Content and briefing questions">
- "Draft a short update for my business partner: our current scores, the trend, and any new mentions this month."
- "Build me a post brief from this week's verified data — the questions I'm absent from and the sources the engines read."
- "What new independent mentions of my business appeared this week?"
</Accordion>

## Full list of available tools

The MCP server exposes 15 tools in total. Fourteen are read-only; one (`request_rescan`) triggers a fresh measurement within your plan's weekly cap and cannot touch your account, plan, or billing.

See [MCP Tools Reference](/mcp/tools) for the complete documentation of all 15 tools, their parameters, and example responses.

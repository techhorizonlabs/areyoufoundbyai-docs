The free scan takes about a minute. It asks two buyer questions on two engines (ChatGPT and Gemini) and shows how often they name your business, who they name instead, the answers behind the result, and a separate check of how well your website is set up for AI. This page walks you through the process from entering your URL to reading your first report.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Go to areyoufoundbyai.com">

  <Step title="Enter your website URL">

  </Step>

  <Step title="See who AI named instead">

  <Step title="Read the questions and answers">

    The report gives you one fix to start with, with **Show me how** for the details, and your website's readiness score out of 100.
  </Step>

## Understanding your first report

### How often AI names you

The headline is the **named share**: countable saved answers that name your business, divided by all countable saved answers. If AI names a business in 7 of 28 countable answers, its named share is 25%.

- Every saved answer counts on its own. An engine may be asked the same question more than once, and each saved answer counts.
- A link to your website on its own does not count as your business being named in the answer.
- Missing or incomplete evidence is left out, never counted as a no.

Read the number with the questions, engines, date and answer count beside it. A short free scan is a smaller sample than a full check, so treat it as a starting point.

### AI Readiness

AI Readiness (out of 100) measures how well your website itself is set up to be understood by AI, from the site alone. It is composed of five site signals: Quotable content (Citability), Trust and credibility (E-E-A-T), Site health (Technical), Machine-readable info (Schema) and AI-ready setup (Platform). A signal that could not be verified is left out rather than scored zero, and each report states the share each signal carries.

Your off-site presence (Wikidata and Knowledge Graph, Google Business Profile, brand mentions, search demand, referring domains) is scored separately as **Footprint**. The free scan does not measure Footprint, so it is shown as not measured rather than zero.

Readiness is the part you control on your own site. It is a separate check, not an extra percentage of AI recommendations, and a good Readiness score does not guarantee that an engine will name you.

### The AI Visibility score in the API and MCP

If you read your result through the [scan API](https://areyoufoundbyai.com/for-agents) or over MCP, you will also see an **AI Visibility** score out of 100. It is an older weighted diagnostic score, not a percentage of AI recommendations, and it cannot be converted into a named share. Being named in the answer prose counts in full toward it; a positive result recorded only as a list entry, citation or source title counts half. Its bands are 60 or more "Visible to AI", 25 or more "Partially visible", and below 25 "Rarely visible".

### What the competitors section shows

The competitors section lists the businesses the engines named when your buyers' questions were asked. Each name comes from a real answer to a real buyer question. It is your list for comparison and "alternatives" content: each competitor is a "you vs them" page worth owning.

### What the answers show

The answers are the evidence behind the number. Read which engines named you, who they named instead and the words behind each result. Being named, being cited as a source and receiving a visit are different outcomes.

The Agency Fleet plan gives you a single console to manage AI visibility monitoring across every client you work with. Your logo goes on every report, your clients see consistent professional output, and your AI assistant can query the entire network in one conversation. This page covers what the Fleet plan includes, how data continuity works, and how to get started.

## Data continuity commitment

Found by AI is designed so that your data is always yours and you are never held hostage to a subscription.

- **Full data export:** Export all measurements as JSON or plain text at any time. You do not need to ask, the export function is always available in your console.
- **Report links stay live:** Shared report URLs remain accessible even after a subscription is cancelled. Clients who bookmarked a report can still open it.
- **Open-source method:** The scoring methodology is open-source at [github.com/techhorizonlabs/thl-open](https://github.com/techhorizonlabs/thl-open). Your team can audit exactly how scores are calculated rather than taking our word for it.
- **Re-parentable networks:** Client networks can be re-parented to a different agency account. If your team structure changes, client data moves with it cleanly.

It is the same measurement we sell everywhere else, under your logo: every client re-measured weekly across all seven answer engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek and Google AI Overviews), with the full answers on record. Your AI assistant can query the whole network in one conversation. This page covers what is included, what you keep if you ever leave, and how to get started.

## What you keep if you leave

Your data is yours, and you are never held hostage to a subscription.

- **Exports, always:** every monitored site's console has a machine-readable export, the full data as JSON and a plain-text context pack, both one click from the sidebar and yours to download at any time.
- **Report links stay live:** report links are standalone documents. Once a report exists, its link keeps working.
- **Nothing is deleted when billing stops:** your network page keeps rendering from the measurement record, report links stay live and your access keys are untouched. Weekly re-measures stop for sites whose Pro subscription lapses; the history they built stays readable and exportable.
- **Open method:** the readiness method and the audit suite are open source at [github.com/techhorizonlabs/thl-open](https://github.com/techhorizonlabs/thl-open). The measurement engine itself is not.
- **Client networks can move:** a client's sites and history belong to the measurement record, keyed by domain. We re-parent client networks on request, and the client's own console links never break in the process.

## What Found by AI Measures

When a buyer asks an AI assistant "who's the best mortgage broker in Brisbane?" or "which software should I use for project management?", does your business get named? Found by AI answers that question with evidence.

  <Card title="AI Visibility Score" icon="eye">

## Get Started in Three Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run a free scan">
    Go to [areyoufoundbyai.com](https://areyoufoundbyai.com) and enter your website URL. The free scan checks 2 buyer questions across 2 AI engines, no account needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your results">
    Your report shows your AI Visibility score, AI Readiness score, which competitors are being named instead of you, and the sources each AI engine cited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix with the plan">
    Each report includes a prioritised fix list. Work through it directly, use the MCP integration with your AI assistant, or use the open-source Claude Code skills.
  </Step>
</Steps>

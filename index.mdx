## What Are you found by AI? measures

When a buyer asks an AI assistant "who's the best mortgage broker in Brisbane?", does your business get named? Are you found by AI? answers that question with the saved answers as evidence.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Named share" icon="eye">
    The headline measure: countable saved answers that name your business, divided by all countable saved answers. Every saved repetition counts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Readiness" icon="gauge">
    How well your website itself is set up to be understood by AI, out of 100, from five site signals. Your off-site Footprint is scored separately.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Saved answers" icon="receipt">
    The answers each engine returned to your buyers' questions, with the sources returned with them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run a free scan">
    Go to [areyoufoundbyai.com](https://areyoufoundbyai.com) and enter your website. The free scan asks 2 buyer questions on 2 engines (ChatGPT and Gemini), with no signup needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your results">
    Your report shows how many saved answers named you, your AI Readiness score, which businesses were named instead of you, and the sources the engines returned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix with the plan">
    Each report includes a prioritised fix plan. Work through it directly, read it with your own AI over MCP, or use the open-source Claude Code skills.
  </Step>
</Steps>

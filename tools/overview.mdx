## What each tool does

**AI Crawler Checker** parses your live robots.txt against 13 AI user-agents (including GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot) and tells you the access status for each one. If a crawler is blocked, you can't be cited by the engine it feeds, no matter how good your content is.

**DNS-AID Checker** looks up the `_index._agents` HTTPS DNS record on your domain. This emerging IETF standard lets AI agents discover your site's machine entry point before crawling a single page. Almost no sites have it yet, which makes adding it a quick differentiator.

**IndexNow Submitter** generates your unique API key, walks you through hosting the one-line key file, and lets you submit any set of URLs directly to Bing's real-time index. Because ChatGPT's web search reads Bing's index, this is the fastest path to getting freshly updated pages discovered.

**llms.txt Generator** crawls your sitemap and produces a formatted `llms.txt` file, a machine-readable map of your most important pages, written in a format AI crawlers read at a fraction of the token cost of crawling your entire site.

**Schema Generator** offers six JSON-LD templates: Organization, Local Business, Article + Author, Product (E-commerce), Software / SaaS, and Website with SearchAction, filled in from your real business details and ready to paste into your server-rendered HTML.

**Chrome Extension** overlays AI visibility data on any page you browse: measured visibility score, whether AI engines name the business, a verbatim excerpt from an engine answer, and a one-click copy of the page as clean Markdown.

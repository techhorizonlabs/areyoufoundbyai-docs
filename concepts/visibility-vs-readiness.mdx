Found by AI produces two scores for every business it measures, and they answer two completely different questions. Your **AI Visibility Score** tells you whether AI engines are naming you in answers right now. Your **AI Readiness Score** tells you whether your site is built to earn those names. Most businesses assume that fixing their website is enough — but the data from 2,354 real scans shows an average readiness of 67/100 and an average visibility of just 40.8/100. Being ready and being recommended are not the same thing, and the gap between them is almost entirely earned off your own site.

## AI Visibility Score

Your AI Visibility Score measures the **outcome**: when real buyers ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, or Google AI Overviews for "the best \[category\] in \[your location\]," does the engine say your name? Found by AI asks those questions across up to seven engines, samples each question multiple times, and majority-votes the results. The score runs from 0 to 100.

| Score range | What it means |
|-------------|---------------|
| 0–10 | Effectively invisible — no engine is naming you |
| 11–30 | Occasionally named on one or two engines |
| 31–60 | Named sometimes — momentum is fragile |
| 61–80 | Named consistently across several engines |
| 81–100 | Strongly recommended across the engine landscape |

A single scan is a point-in-time measurement. Because AI engines are stochastic, the same question can yield a different answer on a different run. Monitoring re-asks your pinned questions every week so you track genuine trends rather than single-run noise.

## AI Readiness Score

Your AI Readiness Score measures the **inputs you control**: is your site and entity built so that AI engines can find, trust, and cite you? It is a composite of six weighted dimensions — see [How the GEO Readiness Score Is Calculated](/concepts/geo-score) for the full formula. The score also runs from 0 to 100.

| Score range | What it means |
|-------------|---------------|
| 0–39 | Critical — fundamental signals are missing |
| 40–59 | Needs Work — foundation gaps are suppressing visibility |
| 60–79 | Good — most signals in place; focus on brand footprint |
| 80–100 | Excellent — well-positioned to earn AI recommendations |

Readiness is what you control directly through site changes, structured data, content strategy, and off-page citations. Visibility is what that work earns you over time.

## Why the Two Scores Diverge

The most important insight in the THL framework is that visibility and readiness are correlated but not identical — and the gap between them tells you exactly what kind of problem you have.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="High Readiness, Low Visibility" icon="clock">
    Your signals are right but the engines haven't fully indexed or trusted you yet. Your site is crawlable, your content is quotable, your schema is valid — but your off-page brand footprint hasn't built the third-party evidence AI needs to confidently name you. This is the most common state: the average business we scan scores 67/100 on readiness and only 40.8/100 on visibility.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Low Readiness, High Visibility" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Your momentum is fragile. You're being named because the wider web already talks about you — reputation is carrying you — but your site's signals don't reinforce that trust. A competitor who outbuilds you on readiness can displace you as engines update their training data and retrieval indexes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The Two Scores Side by Side

| | AI Visibility Score | AI Readiness Score |
|--|--|--|
| **Question answered** | Are you named by AI engines right now? | Is your site built to earn AI recommendations? |
| **What it measures** | Live engine answers — verbatim, majority-voted | 30+ signals across 6 weighted dimensions |
| **Primary drivers** | Off-page brand recognition, third-party mentions, entity authority | Citability, Brand signals, E-E-A-T, Technical GEO, Schema, Platform |
| **How to improve it** | Earn citations, build entity footprint, get listed on AI-trusted directories | Fix crawlability, publish quotable content, add valid JSON-LD, open AI crawlers |
| **How fast it moves** | Weeks to months (engine retrieval and training cycles) | Days to weeks (site changes are measurable immediately) |
| **Measured by** | Querying the 7 engines live with buyer questions | Auditing your site and entity against the GEO readiness model |

## The 3-Layer THL Method

<Note>
Found by AI structures its workflow around three layers that follow directly from this distinction. Run them in order to move from outcome to diagnosis to fix.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Measure — areyoufoundbyai.com">
    Run a live scan to get your AI Visibility Score. This tells you the outcome: are you being named, who's being named instead, and on which engines? This is the only way to know — on-page signals predict visibility but don't prove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Diagnose — GEO Readiness Audit">
    Your AI Readiness Score breaks down across 6 dimensions and surfaces the specific signals holding you back. This is the "why" — it tells you which lever to pull first, with fixes ordered by impact and effort.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix — Implement and Re-measure">
    Apply the prioritised fixes from your report. Monitoring re-asks your pinned buyer questions every week so that when a change ships, you see exactly which questions moved and on which engines — the change you made, confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
